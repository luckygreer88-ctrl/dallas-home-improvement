Why It Matters

What Clogged Gutters Actually Cost You

Protects Your Foundation

Overflowing gutters dump water right next to your foundation instead of routing it away, which over time can lead to cracks and costly structural repairs.

Prevents Roof Damage

Standing water and trapped debris on the roofline can rot fascia boards and work its way under shingles, leading to leaks inside the home.

Keeps Pests Away

Wet, leaf-packed gutters are a favorite nesting spot for mosquitoes, birds, and rodents. Clearing them out removes the invitation.

Protects Your Landscaping

Overflow erodes flower beds, mulch, and soil right under the gutter line. Clear-flowing gutters keep water where it belongs.

Common Questions

Gutter Cleaning FAQ

How often do gutters need to be cleaned?

Most Dallas homes do well with cleaning twice a year — spring and fall. Homes surrounded by mature trees may need a third visit, especially after heavy leaf drop.

What happens if I skip gutter cleaning?

Clogged gutters overflow, and that water has to go somewhere — usually down your fascia boards, siding, or foundation, leading to rot, cracked paint, and costly water damage over time.

Do you clean the downspouts too?

Yes. A clean gutter trough doesn't help if the downspout is blocked, so we check and clear flow through every downspout on the job.

Is gutter cleaning safe for two-story homes?

Yes — our crew is equipped and experienced working on single- and two-story homes throughout the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex.
