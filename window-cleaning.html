Double-hung, transom, arched, casement, and glass door windows count as 1. Sliding windows, door sidelights, and sliding glass doors count as 2. Picture, bay, and bow windows count as 3. French doors count as 1, though additional charges may apply.

Does a window with two panes count as two windows?

No. A standard double-hung window counts as one window even though it has an upper and lower pane.

Does a sliding glass door count as one window?

No. A sliding glass door counts as two windows.

How are bay, bow, and picture windows counted?

Bay, bow, and picture windows each count as three windows because of their size and multiple sections of glass.

Do glass doors count as windows?

Yes. A standard door with glass counts as one window. Sliding glass doors count as two, and French doors count as one with possible additional charges.

Do door sidelights count as windows?

Yes. Door sidelights, the narrow windows beside an entry door, count as two windows.

Hard Water Spots & Window Problems

Can you remove hard water spots from my windows?

Yes, in most cases, and hard water spot removal is included at no extra charge. Spots that have been left on the glass for many years can etch into the glass permanently, and we'll tell you honestly if that's the case.

What causes white spots on my windows?

White spots are usually minerals left behind when hard water dries on the glass. In the Dallas area, the most common cause is lawn sprinklers spraying the windows.

Why won't regular glass cleaner remove the spots on my windows?

Household glass cleaner removes dirt and fingerprints, but it doesn't dissolve the mineral deposits left by hard water. Those spots need a treatment designed to break down minerals, which is included with our window cleaning.

How can I stop sprinklers from spotting my windows?

Adjust or redirect sprinkler heads so they don't spray the house, and water early in the morning. Having your windows cleaned regularly also keeps minerals from building up and etching the glass.

Why do my windows look foggy between the panes?

Fog or moisture between the two panes of a double-pane window usually means the window's seal has failed. Cleaning can't fix that because the moisture is sealed inside the glass, so the window would need to be repaired or replaced by a window company.

Why do my windows get dirty so fast in Dallas?

North Texas wind, dust, spring pollen, rain spotting, and sprinkler overspray all build up on windows quickly. That's why many Dallas homeowners clean their windows at least once or twice a year.

Window Cleaning Tips from Our Team

How to Remove Hard Water Spots from Windows

Hard water spots are mineral deposits, usually from sprinklers, and they need an acid-based cleaner to dissolve them. Regular glass cleaner won't work.

  1. Mix equal parts white vinegar and warm water in a spray bottle.
  2. Spray the spotted area and let it sit for 5–10 minutes so the vinegar can break down the minerals.
  3. Gently wipe with a soft microfiber cloth or non-scratch sponge.
  4. Rinse with clean water and dry with a clean microfiber cloth.
  5. Repeat on stubborn spots. Never use steel wool, scouring pads, or razor blades, which can scratch the glass or damage window tint.

When to call a pro: if spots are widespread, on second-story windows, or don't come off after a few tries. Hard water spot removal is included free with our window cleaning.

How to Prevent Sprinkler Stains on Windows

The best way to prevent sprinkler stains is to stop the water from reaching the glass.

  1. Walk around the house while your sprinklers run and note which heads hit the windows.
  2. Adjust the spray direction or arc so water lands on the lawn, not the house.
  3. Lower the pressure or swap in lower-angle nozzles near the house.
  4. Water early in the morning so less water sits and dries on the glass in the sun.
  5. Clean windows regularly so minerals don't build up and etch the glass.

When to call a pro: if your windows already have heavy spotting. Removal is included free with our cleaning.
