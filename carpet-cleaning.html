Common Questions

Carpet Cleaning FAQ

How often should carpets be professionally cleaned?

Every 12-18 months for most households, or every 6-12 months if you have kids, pets, or allergy concerns. Regular cleaning also extends the life of your carpet.

How long does it take carpet to dry?

With our low-moisture hot water extraction process, most carpets are dry within 4-6 hours. Good airflow — open windows or a ceiling fan — speeds things up.

Can you remove old, set-in stains?

We can significantly lighten most stains with pretreatment and targeted spotting, though very old stains that have permanently altered the fiber may not come out 100%. We'll always give you an honest expectation upfront.

Do you move furniture before cleaning?

We can move light furniture like chairs and small tables. For larger pieces, we ask that they be cleared ahead of time so we can clean the full area underneath.
