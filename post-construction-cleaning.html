Common Questions

Post-Construction & House Cleaning FAQ

How soon after construction finishes can you clean?

As soon as the crew has cleared out and major debris is removed, we can come in for the detail clean. Many customers schedule us for the day after the final walkthrough so the space is ready to move into right away.

Will you remove leftover construction debris?

We handle fine dust, residue, and small leftover debris (packaging, small offcuts). Large debris removal like drywall scraps or lumber is typically handled by the contractor before we come in.

Do you offer one-time or recurring house cleaning?

Both. Many customers start with a one-time deep clean after a renovation, then switch to recurring visits (weekly, biweekly, or monthly) for ongoing upkeep.

What should I do to prepare for the cleaning crew?

Just make sure the space is accessible and any items you want kept are out of the way. We handle the rest — dust, surfaces, floors, and detail work.
