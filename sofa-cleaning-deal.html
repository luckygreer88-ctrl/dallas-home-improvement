Most upholstery is dry to the touch within 4–6 hours with fans or good airflow, and fully dry within 24 hours.

Can you get out pet stains and odors?

In most cases, yes. Targeted pretreatment plus hot water extraction lifts embedded stains and odors. Very old, set-in stains may lighten rather than fully disappear.
