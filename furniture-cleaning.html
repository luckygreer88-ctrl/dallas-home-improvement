Common Questions

Furniture Cleaning FAQ

How long until my furniture is dry and usable again?

Most upholstery is dry to the touch within 4-6 hours with fans or good airflow, and fully cured within 24 hours. We use low-moisture methods to speed this up.

Is the cleaning process safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We use pet- and family-safe cleaning solutions, and can point out any areas that should stay off-limits until fully dry.

Can you get rid of pet odors and stains?

In most cases, yes. Our hot water extraction process combined with targeted pretreatment lifts embedded odors and stains that surface cleaning can't reach — though very old, set-in stains may lighten rather than fully disappear.

Do you clean fabric and leather?

We specialize in fabric upholstery — sofas, chairs, and mattresses. Ask us when you call and we'll let you know what's the best fit for your piece.
