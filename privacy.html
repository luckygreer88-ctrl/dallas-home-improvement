Privacy Policy

How Dallas Home Improvement Cleaning Services collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This is a general-purpose privacy policy template provided as a starting point. It is not legal advice. We recommend having a licensed attorney review it to make sure it fully matches how your business actually collects and uses data.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us by phone, text message, or through this website, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, service address, and details about the service you're requesting.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to quote requests and schedule appointments
  • Communicate with you about your service, including confirmations and reminders
  • Provide the cleaning services you request
  • Improve our services and customer experience

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. SMS / Text Messaging

By texting us or clicking the "Text Us" button on this website, you consent to receive text messages from Dallas Home Improvement Cleaning Services related to your quote request or service appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You can opt out of text communications at any time by replying "STOP" to any message, or by calling us at (469) 562-1010. For help, reply "HELP" or contact us directly.

4. Embedded Third-Party Content

This website embeds videos hosted on YouTube. When you view a page with an embedded video, YouTube (Google LLC) may collect data about your visit in accordance with its own privacy policy. We do not control this data collection. You can review Google's privacy practices at their official privacy policy page.

5. Cookies & Website Data

This website may use basic cookies or similar technologies to support standard website functionality and, if enabled, to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Advertising & Analytics Tracking

This website uses advertising and analytics tracking technologies, including the OpenAI Ads measurement pixel and analytics tools, to measure how well our ads perform and how visitors use the site. These tools may collect information such as the pages you view, the links you tap (for example, calling or texting us), your device and browser type, and your approximate location. We use this information to understand and improve our advertising and website. We do not sell sensitive personal information.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services and website are intended for adults seeking home cleaning services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, call or text us at (469) 562-1010.
